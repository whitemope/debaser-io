*Strictly confidential. July 2026.*

## 01. Cover

**The intelligence layer for music royalties.**

Debaser finds errors, explains payments and helps rights teams recover missing income.

---

## 02. The problem

**Royalty teams cannot see the full picture.**

Contracts sit in PDFs. Catalogue data is inconsistent. Statements arrive in different formats. Existing systems calculate what they receive, but rarely tell teams what is missing, wrong or worth investigating.

The result is manual work, slow answers and lost income.

---

## 03. The gap

**The market does not need another royalty calculator.**

Curve, Vistex and internal systems remain the system of record.

Debaser sits above them.

It connects contracts, catalogue data, statements and existing royalty systems. It checks the work, explains the result and turns problems into action.

---

## 04. The product

**One workspace for royalty intelligence.**

| Step | What Debaser does |
|---|---|
| Connect | Ingest statements, contracts, catalogue data and system exports. |
| Match | Link income to works, recordings, parties and agreements. |
| Investigate | Find gaps, conflicts, duplicates and unusual changes. |
| Explain | Trace every finding to a row, clause or source file. |
| Resolve | Prepare corrections, claims and tasks for approval. |

---

## 05. The first use case

**Find mistakes and missing money before the next royalty run.**

A customer provides one quarter of statements, catalogue data and contracts.

Debaser returns:

1. Unmatched income.
2. Missing or conflicting splits.
3. Unexpected changes.
4. Contract errors.
5. Claim-ready evidence.

**Entry offer:** Send us last quarter. We will show you what is wrong.

---

## 06. Why customers buy

**The value is measurable.**

Debaser can:

1. Recover income.
2. Reduce manual reconciliation.
3. Shorten artist and client queries.
4. Improve confidence before payments go out.
5. Preserve royalty knowledge when staff leave.

The commercial test is simple. Debaser should recover or save more than it costs.

---

## 07. Why now

**Royalty complexity keeps growing.**

More platforms, territories and revenue types create more data and more failure points.

At the same time, software can now read contracts, compare large data sets and produce evidence-backed findings.

The source systems were built to calculate. The next layer will investigate.

---

## 08. Competitive position

**Debaser works with the existing stack.**

| Product type | Primary job |
|---|---|
| Curve, Vistex, Counterpoint | Royalty accounting and statements. |
| Reprtoir, Revelator | Catalogue and music operations. |
| Auditors and royalty specialists | Manual investigation and recovery. |
| **Debaser** | Continuous investigation, explanation and action across all of the above. |

This creates a faster route into customers and avoids a full system migration.

**Lead with proof, not a software demo.**

1. Recruit three to five design partners.
2. Run a focused historical or quarterly audit.
3. Show recovered income and time saved.
4. Convert the workflow into an annual contract.
5. Expand across catalogues, territories and teams.

Initial customers:

Independent labels. Publishers. Label services companies. Catalogue funds. Artist managers. Neighbouring rights specialists.

---

## 11. Product foundation

**Accuracy comes from structure and evidence.**

Debaser combines:

1. Connectors to royalty systems and source data.
2. A rights graph linking songs, recordings, contracts, ownership and income.
3. Rules for calculations and approvals.
4. Models for reading, matching and investigation.
5. A full audit trail for every finding.

Models investigate. Rules control. People approve.

---

## 12. Defensibility

**The moat is the rights and operations graph.**

Each workflow improves Debaser's understanding of:

1. How rights are represented.
2. How contracts affect payments.
3. Where income commonly goes missing.
4. How claims are resolved.
5. Which sources can be trusted.

Over time, Debaser becomes the operating memory for royalty teams.

---

## 13. Long-term vision

**From royalty intelligence to royalty infrastructure.**

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Audit existing royalty data and recover missing income. |
| 2 | Become the shared rights and contract layer across systems. |
| 3 | Run selected royalty workflows and calculations. |
| 4 | Add usage data from platforms, partners and venue sensors. |
| 5 | Support direct claims, administration and collection. |

The long-term opportunity is a clearer route from music usage to the correct rights holder.

Hardware may support venue detection later. It is not required for the first product.
