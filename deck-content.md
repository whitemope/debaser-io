## 02. The Problem

**The economy of music catalogue management is fragmented.**

Billion-dollar catalogue funds now own hundreds of thousands of songs. Arts IP whales are buying at a pace the back office can't match. And every independent label and publisher underneath them inherits the same broken workflow. Just at a different scale.

| # | Problem | Detail |
|---|---------|--------|
| 01 | Messy statements | Incompatible formats from every DSP, CMO and distributor. |
| 02 | Bad metadata | Missing ISRCs, misspelled artists, duplicate works. |
| 03 | Contract complexity | Rates, splits, recoupment buried in PDFs no system reads. |
| 04 | Unmatched income | Revenue sitting unattributed in royalty pools. |
| 05 | Artist disputes | Payment questions that take days to answer. If ever. |
| 06 | Manual reconciliation | Spreadsheet hell. Every quarter. Every time. |

## 03. Why Now

**Three converging tailwinds.**

1. **$31.7bn. Music revenue growing**
   Recorded music hit $31.7bn in 2025, up 6.4% YoY. The rights economy keeps expanding. So does the ops complexity behind it.

2. **×7. Royalty complexity exploding**
   Streaming, UGC, short-form, sync, social, live, neighbouring rights. Each with different rules, rates and reporting standards.

3. **Now. AI is finally capable**
   LLMs can extract, match and explain at the level royalty ops demands. But royalty systems are still mostly workflow and accounting tools.

**Capital is already betting big on catalogues:**
- **Recognition Music Group (ex-Hipgnosis):** Blackstone took the fund private for $1.6bn in 2024. Sony Music Publishing bought its 45,000-song Recognition catalogue in 2026.
- **Primary Wave:** Closed a $2.225bn fourth flagship fund in 2025. The largest dedicated closed-end music royalty fund raised to date.
- **Concord:** Backed an $850m securitisation across 1m+ songs, after acquiring Round Hill's catalogue fund for ~$469m.
- **Pophouse:** Raised $1.3bn in 2025 to acquire catalogues and build experiences around them.

*Sources: Billboard, Music Business Worldwide, Music Week, Variety (2024 to 2026).*

## 10. Agentic Layer (Architecture)

**Agentic by design.**

| Agent | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Ingestion Agent | Maps messy CSVs, PDFs and portal exports into a standard royalty schema, regardless of source format. |
| Contract Agent | Extracts splits, rates, territories, deductions, recoupment and exceptions directly from contract documents. |
| Matching Agent | Links income to ISRCs, ISWCs, artists, writers, labels, works and recordings across the rights catalogue. |
| Exception Agent | Finds duplicates, missing splits, unexplained drops, expired contracts and unmatched income rows. |
| Audit Agent | Compares expected income against received statements and prepares claim evidence by territory. |
| Ops Agent | Turns every royalty run into a prioritised action list your team can close before payments go out. |

**AI investigates. Humans approve. Deterministic engines calculate.**
